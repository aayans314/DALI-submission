# Demo walkthrough

Every account uses the password `demo1234`. Staff log in under "I work at a shelter". Public users log in under "I'm looking for someone", then tap "I already have an account".

Staff emails follow the pattern `<shelter>.<role>@demo.org`. The shelters are `lincoln`, `riverside`, `stbrendan` and `fairgrounds`, and the roles are `authority` (the coordinator), `vol1` and `vol2`. St. Brendan's only has `vol1`. Public users are `elena@`, `kevin@`, `priya@`, `travis@` and `tom@demo.org`.

## Already in motion when you log in

**A son looking for his mother, who was registered twice.** Kevin searched for "Margaret O'Neill / Peggy / Maggie". She was moved from a nursing home to Riverside, then to the Fairgrounds, where the night shift registered her again as "Peggy Oneil".

1. Log in as `fairgrounds.vol1`. Home shows a match to ask about and a duplicate to review.
2. Open **Matches**: both of her records are candidates for Kevin's search. Tap **They want to connect**.
3. Log in as `kevin@`. His search shows where she is.
4. Back as staff, open **Duplicates** and merge the two records. Her shelter history combines into one chain.

**A husband rescued two days late, searched for by a wife with no phone.** A Lincoln volunteer filed a search for Grace Okafor, looking for David. David is at St. Brendan's.

1. Log in as `stbrendan.vol1`, open **Matches**, and tap **They want to connect**. The volunteer now sees that Grace is at Lincoln High School Gym.
2. Log in as `lincoln.vol2`. Home shows "1 search for people here found someone", and Grace's record shows where David is.

**Someone who doesn't want to be found.** Travis searched for Dana Whitfield, who is marked private at Riverside. Her match was declined automatically.

1. Log in as `riverside.vol1`. No volunteer is ever asked about this match.
2. Log in as `travis@`. He sees "Still looking", exactly what an empty search looks like.

## Try these yourself

**A child bused from school.** Log in as `elena@` and search for "Lucas Ramirez" and "Luke", age 8–10. He's registered at Lincoln as "Lucas Ramires".

1. Log in as `lincoln.vol1` and tap **They want to connect**. Because he's a minor, the match goes to the coordinator.
2. Log in as `lincoln.authority` and approve it. Elena now sees where Lucas is.

**A typo and a nickname.** Log in as `priya@` and search for "Rajesh Shah" and "Raj Shah", age 25–30. He's registered at Lincoln as "Raj Sha".

**Nobody to find.** Log in as `tom@` and search for "Eleanor Becker". The search stays open. Register an Eleanor Becker at any shelter, and Tom's search matches her on the spot.

**A man who walked between shelters.** As `stbrendan.vol1`, open **Duplicates**: "Jon Peirce" here is "Jonathan Pierce", who was registered at Riverside. Merging moves his record to St. Brendan's and closes the Riverside stay.

**The same name, two different people.** There are two Michael Johnsons, aged 22–25 and 60–65. They are never flagged as duplicates.

**Records with little to go on.** "Unknown" at the Fairgrounds has only an age range and a description. "Maria" at St. Brendan's gave a first name only. Harold Kim moved twice and then left the shelters.
